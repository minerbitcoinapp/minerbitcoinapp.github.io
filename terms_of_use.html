6. WITHDRAWALS AND LIGHTNING NETWORK

Withdrawal Method

Withdrawals are processed exclusively via the Bitcoin Lightning Network using:

  • A valid BOLT11 Lightning invoice (lnbc...); or
  • A valid Lightning address (user@domain.com).

Withdrawal Conditions

  • Minimum withdrawal: 10 Satoshi;
  • Maximum withdrawal: 1,000,000 Satoshi per transaction;
  • Withdrawals are processed automatically upon request;
  • Lightning invoices have limited validity — expired invoices will result in failed withdrawals.

Failed Withdrawals

Withdrawals may fail due to:

  • Invalid or expired Lightning invoice;
  • Lightning Network routing failures;
  • Invalid Lightning address;
  • Insufficient liquidity on the Lightning Network;
  • Technical issues.

In the event of a failed withdrawal, the Satoshi amount will be automatically refunded to your in-app balance. We are not responsible for delays or losses caused by Lightning Network infrastructure outside our control.

Withdrawal Processing

We do not guarantee the speed of Lightning Network payments. While Lightning payments are typically near-instant, processing times depend on network conditions beyond our control.
