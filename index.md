# Bitcoin Miner

Bitcoin Miner is a free Android app by **Serenity Games**. You watch short rewarded
ads, your simulated mining farm produces satoshis, and you withdraw them to your own
Lightning wallet.

- **Get the app:** [Bitcoin Miner on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitcoinminer.app.dev)
- **Privacy Policy:** [minerbitcoinapp.github.io/PRIVACY_POLICY](https://minerbitcoinapp.github.io/PRIVACY_POLICY)
- **Terms of Use:** [minerbitcoinapp.github.io/terms_of_use.html](https://minerbitcoinapp.github.io/terms_of_use.html)
- **Contact:** [mine.bitcoin.app@gmail.com](mailto:mine.bitcoin.app@gmail.com)

## What the app does

- Watch a rewarded ad to add a miner. Each miner runs for 24 hours.
- A daily bonus and offset ads keep your mining efficiency up.
- Withdraw your satoshis over the Lightning Network to any Lightning address or
  invoice, from 20 satoshis upwards.

Mining in the app is a **simulation**: there is no real mining hardware. Satoshis
come from advertising revenue, and the amounts are small.

## Why the app asks for your Google account

Bitcoin Miner asks you to sign in with Google for one reason: to tie your balance and
your payouts to an account that is yours, so that your satoshis survive a reinstall or
a new phone, and so that payouts cannot be claimed by someone else.

From your Google account we use only your **email address and account ID**. We do not
read your contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data, and we never sell or
share your data with advertisers. What we store and why is listed in full in our
[Privacy Policy](https://minerbitcoinapp.github.io/PRIVACY_POLICY).

## Rules

One person, one account. One wallet per account. Multiple accounts, automation,
emulators or modified versions of the app lead to a permanent block without payout.
