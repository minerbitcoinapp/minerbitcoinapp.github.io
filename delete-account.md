# Delete your account and data

Bitcoin Miner (published by **Serenity Games**) lets you delete your account and the
data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

## 1. In the app (fastest)

1. Open **Bitcoin Miner**.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. If you still have a balance, withdraw it first —
deleting the account does not pay it out.

Deletion is blocked for 24 hours after a payout, so that the payment can be completed
and checked. Please try again after 24 hours.

## 2. By email (no app needed)

Write to **[mine.bitcoin.app@gmail.com](mailto:mine.bitcoin.app@gmail.com)** from the
email address you use in the app, with the subject **Delete my account**. We delete the
account within 30 days and reply when it is done. If your account is blocked and you
cannot delete it yourself, use this route.

## What is deleted

- Your user record: balance, earnings, mining state, username, Lightning address,
  ad-verification records, and the anti-fraud device fingerprint.
- Your sign-in (Google account link) with our app.

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Payout records** (amount, time, status, payment hash) for up to **24 months**. We
  are required to keep a record of payments we made, and it also protects us against
  repeated fraud. These records keep the internal account number, not your email.
- **A deletion marker** with the internal account number, the date, and a one-way
  hash of your email and wallet address, so a deleted account cannot be recreated to
  bypass a block. The hash cannot be turned back into your email or address.

Everything else is removed and is not recoverable.
